How to Play

The goal of the game is to uncover all safe tiles on a grid without clicking on a mine. Most tiles are safe, but some hide mines underneath.

When you reveal a tile it will either be blank (meaning no mines are nearby), a number from 1–8 (how many mines are adjacent to the square), or a mine (ends the game).

Left click a tile to reveal it. Press the flag button to place flags on tiles you think hide mines.

You win when every safe tile has been revealed, and you lose when you click a mine.

The number of rounds you win in a row for any difficulty is tracked, try to get it as high as possible! To reset your streak, press the Reset button below.
